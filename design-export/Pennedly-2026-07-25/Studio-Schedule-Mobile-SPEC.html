Pennedly · Mobile spec · Studio — publish-later

Scheduling /app — phone

The phone version of Studio scheduling. The “schedule for later” entry moves onto the ready card’s action footer — a labelled Schedule button beside Publish — and the confirm dialog becomes a bottom sheet: the Publish now / Schedule control, then Date + Time stacked, with full-width actions (primary on top). Once scheduled, the card shows a “Goes out …” foot + accent badge and a way into the Calendar. Card actions stay thumb-reachable (≥44px); inputs are ≥16px. Same states and copy as desktop — only the layout adapts below 600px. Every frame is the real component layer at 390px, light and dark.

studio-schedule.css calendar.css pennedly-mobile.css StudioParts.tsx ds/tokens.css
What doesn’t change. Publish-now stays one tap; scheduling requires ≥ now + 5 min and echoes local time as UTC. Reschedule / unschedule / retry live on the Calendar — Studio links out. No new icon. The dialog → bottom sheet is the only structural change.

1Entry point on the card

The ready card foot: a labelled Schedule (clock) beside the primary Publish, with the 44px ⋯ overflow. “Schedule for later” is visible up front; Publish stays the one-tap common case. Publish opens the dialog (sheet) in Publish-now mode; Schedule opens it straight in Schedule mode.

Three controls in one footer — the fit. The two-tier foot keeps the shipped idiom: a meta line (159 / 500 · ✓ Ready) on top, then the action row — ⋯ (44×44) + Schedule (grows) + Publish (grows). Both buttons are ≥44px tall and split the remaining width evenly (~135px each at 390, ~120 at 360). On long locales (DE/RU) the labels wrap to a second line rather than clip or push ⋯ off-screen. Schedule stays labelled — an icon-only control would re-hide the feature we’re surfacing. The ⋯ overflow still holds the shipped secondary actions (Edit · Send back · Translate).

2Sheet · Publish now

The confirm becomes a bottom sheet over the card. Publish now mode: account + text + the now/schedule control; actions full-width, primary on top.

3Sheet · Schedule (empty)

Schedule mode: Date + Time stacked, a ≥5-min hint, and the Schedule primary disabled until valid.

4Sheet · Schedule (valid)

Filled with a valid time: “= 14:30 UTC · at least 5 min out”; the primary becomes “Schedule for Sat, Jun 16 · 14:30”.

5Sheet · Schedule (too soon)

A time < 5 min out: danger hint, Schedule disabled, picked time persists.

6Card · Scheduled

Accent “Scheduled” badge + “Goes out Sat, Jun 16, 14:30” foot; a full-width “Manage on Calendar” affordance + the ⋯-menu (read-only in Studio — routes to the Calendar).

7Tab · Scheduled

The Studio status filter scrolls horizontally; the new “Scheduled” tab (accent dot) sits between Ready and Published.

8Tab · Scheduled (empty)

The empty-state for the Scheduled tab, with a way into the Calendar.

✓Checklist

  • Dialog → bottom sheet: now/schedule control, stacked Date + Time, full-width actions (primary on top).
  • Schedule empty → disabled; valid → “= 14:30 UTC · at least 5 min out” + enabled; too soon → danger hint + disabled.
  • Scheduled card: accent badge + “Goes out …” foot + Manage-on-Calendar + ⋯ (read-only, routes out).
  • Scheduled tab between Ready and Published (accent dot); + per-tab empty state.
  • Reschedule / unschedule / retry linked to the Calendar, not rebuilt.
  • ≥ now + 5 min; local echoed as UTC; numbers tabular; labels wrap.
  • Inputs ≥16px, targets ≥44px; correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour; no new icon.
