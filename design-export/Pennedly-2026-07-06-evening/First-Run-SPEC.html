Pennedly · Spec · First-run & import states

Importing your history · first-run analytics

When someone connects a Threads account, Pennedly backfills their posts, per-post metrics and comments in the background — seconds to a few minutes. Today there's no sign of it, and Stats collapses to a single “come back in two weeks” card when there are zero posts yet. This spec fixes both: a reusable “Importing your history” banner + skeleton that shows live progress across Dashboard, Stats and Replies; a progressive Stats page that always shows the four real summary cards (even small or zero) plus a warm nudge instead of an empty wall; and honest empty-states for the few metrics Threads genuinely can't backfill. All end-user copy is English (source locale); it localizes to the 8 app locales afterward. Frames below are live — real DS components, light and dark, at screen width.

+ import-banner.css + stats-firstrun.css firstrun-spec.js stats.css stats-followers.css ds/tokens.css ds/components.css
Live frames are the real system layer. This doc links ds/tokens.css + ds/components.css + stats.css + stats-followers.css + the two new layers and renders components exactly as the product does. Each frame pins its own tokens: light from :root, dark via the dark class — so both themes sit side by side without touching the doc page. Nothing is hardcoded; every colour is a semantic token.
The core principle. A freshly-connected account must never read as empty or broken. We always show what's real (even tiny numbers), we say plainly what's still loading, and for the handful of metrics Threads can't give us yet, we explain why in one warm sentence rather than hiding the panel or apologizing.

1What this covers

Three deliverables, one feature: the first few minutes of a connected account. The banner is shared; the progressive page and honest empty-states live on Stats (the follower honest-states also apply anywhere the follower panel renders).

Deliverable1 · reusable “Importing your history” banner + skeleton · 2 · progressive Stats first-run page · 3 · honest empty-states for un-backfillable metrics.
Where the banner showsDashboard, Stats and Replies — top of the content column, full content width — while a just-connected account is still syncing. Removed the moment the import resolves.
Why a backfill existsOn connect we pull the account's posts, per-post metrics and comments from Threads in the background. It's fast (seconds–minutes) but not instant, so the screens need a truthful “loading” story rather than looking broken.
What Threads can't backfillFollower history (the API gives only today's count → growth starts from today); follower analytics below ~100 followers (gated by Threads); viral-tier classification until there are enough posts to form a baseline (shown as “Calculating…”).
ToneWarm, non-apologetic. “The platform is working, your history is just loading.” No error styling, no “sorry”, no dead-ends.
AccessibilityBanner progress sub is an aria-live="polite" region so the climbing counts are announced. Skeletons are aria-hidden. Spinner + sweep respect prefers-reduced-motion.

2Import banner + skeleton import-banner.css

A calm, full-width banner that announces the background import and shows live progress as data lands. Built from tokens only; light and dark below.

2.2 — States

Just started (indeterminate)Importing (live counts)Imported most (partial)Resolved (removed)

The banner walks left → right as the job runs. Just started: counts unknown, indeterminate sweep. Importing: live post/comment counts + a determinate bar. Imported most: a quieter success-rail note for a partial import (older posts still trickling in) — bar drops away, the counts carry the message. Resolved: the banner unmounts and the normal screen shows (no placeholder left behind).

StateTriggerWhat renders
Just startedjob queued, no counts yetSpinner + “Pulling your posts, metrics and comments from Threads…” + indeterminate sweep.
Importingcounts streaming inSpinner + “N posts · M comments so far” + determinate bar (imported ÷ total).
Imported most≥ ~95% done, tail remaining--partial: check glyph, success rail, “Imported most of your history” + “A few older posts are still finishing up.” No bar.
Resolvedimport completeBanner removed entirely; the normal screen renders. Optional one-shot success toast (“History imported”).
Determinate vs indeterminate. The bar fills proportionally only once the import job reports a known post total. Before that it sweeps — honest about not-yet-knowing rather than faking a percentage.

2.3 — In context: banner + skeleton

The skeleton is the reusable other half: while the first rows of data are still landing, each screen renders a placeholder that mirrors its destination layout 1:1, with the banner pinned on top. Real content swaps in row-by-row with no jump. Below — Stats and Replies, both mid-sync.

ScreenSkeleton mirrors
DashboardOverview tiles + recent-activity rows (same grid as the live overview).
Stats4 .stat-card placeholders + a tall trend-panel placeholder (the destination grid).
RepliesComment rows — avatar circle + two/three text lines per row.
Progressive, not blocking. As soon as any real data has landed, screens prefer showing it (with the banner still on top) over a full skeleton. The skeleton is only for the brief window before the first rows arrive.

3Stats first-run / progressive stats-firstrun.css

The all-or-nothing “come back in two weeks” wall is gone. We always render the four real summary cards — at whatever they are, even small or zero — and put a warm nudge below them. The page is alive from the first post.

3.1 — Before / after

What's replaced. The old .stats-empty wall (“Not enough data yet… at least two weeks of activity”) hid the whole page behind a single card and read as broken. The progressive page keeps the real numbers visible and explains the rest inline.

3.2 — The progressive page

Top to bottom: import banner (while syncing) → 4 real summary cards → warm nudge → follower panel (honest state) → performance spread (calculating). Deltas read “no prior period” on a fresh account — there's no equal-length window behind it yet, which is the truth, not an error.

Element / classDescription
.stats-grid / .stat-card ×4Unchanged from Stats — Posts · Views · Likes · Comments, each number --text-h1. First-run sub-lines read “since you connected” / “N avg / post”; delta = “no prior period”.
.stats-nudgeThe calm replacement for the wall: flex; gap 14; surface-2; border; radius-lg; padding 16/18. Mark (38×38) + title + sub (≤64ch). Two messages — see below.
.sn-title / .sn-subWhile syncing: “Your history is importing” + “These numbers are real and growing as we pull the rest…”. Once done but small: “You're all set up” + a first-step prompt + an “Open the Studio” button.
Follower panel.fchart-collect honest state (§4) — never the flat 0-based line.
Performance spread.distrow--calc “Calculating…” until a baseline exists (§4).

3.3 — Zero & import-done variants

Even with zero posts, the page is honest, not broken — cards read 0 with “no posts yet”, and the nudge carries the story. Once the import finishes on a genuinely small account, the nudge switches to a first-step prompt rather than nagging about a waiting period.

VariantNudge
Importing“Your history is importing” — numbers are real and growing; nothing is missing.
Zero posts yetSame importing nudge; cards show 0 with “no posts yet”. No wall, no error.
Done · small account“You're all set up” + first-step prompt + “Open the Studio”. No two-week countdown.

4Honest empty-states things Threads can't backfill

A few metrics can't be pulled from history — so we say so, warmly and specifically, instead of hiding the panel or faking data. Each reuses the existing .fchart-collect / spread chrome so it reads as calm, not as an error.

NEW4.1 — Follower growth · < 2 days of data

Threads gives only today's follower count, not its history — so there's no line to draw yet. The header still shows the current count (it's real); the plot area carries the explanation. Left frame below; the locked case is §4.2.

StateCopy
Collecting (<2 days)Title: “Growth starts tracking today”. Sub: “Threads only gives your current follower count, not its history — so we start plotting your growth from today. Check back tomorrow for the first move.” Header shows the real count + “growth from today”.
Tracking (≥2 days)The normal min–max line (shown for contrast below). The block is independent of the period selector — always full history since connect.

NEW4.2 — Follower count not available yet

Threads only shares follower analytics once an account passes about 100 followers. Below that we can't show the count at all — so we name the threshold and reassure that the rest of the page still works. (Shown as the right frame in §4.1 above.)

ElementDetail
.fchart-collect--lockedSame calm dashed chrome as collecting, with a lock glyph. Header count shows “—” (genuinely unavailable).
CopyTitle: “Follower insights unlock at ~100 followers”. Sub: “Threads only shares follower analytics once an account passes about 100 followers. You're at 64 — almost there, and everything else on this page works in the meantime.”

NEW4.3 — Viral tiers · Calculating…

Viral tiers classify each post against your own baseline (× your average). On a fresh account there aren't enough posts to form that baseline yet — so the panel keeps its tier scale visible but shows a calm “Calculating…” in place of counts, rather than a misleading all-zeros chart.

Element / classDescription
.calc-pillPanel-header pill: “Calculating” + three pulsing dots (dot-pulse, no spinner). Subtle colour — quiet, not urgent.
.distrow--calcEach tier keeps its dot + name + sub (the scale stays legible). The count/percent and the proportion bar are replaced by a calm shimmer (.sfr-skel) until the baseline is ready.
Resolves toThe normal .distlist with real counts + bars once there are enough posts (~5+) to compare against.

6Implementation checklist

  • Import banner reusable across Dashboard / Stats / Replies; top of content column, full width; removed on resolve.
  • States: just-started (indeterminate sweep) · importing (live counts + determinate bar) · imported-most (success rail + check, no bar) · resolved (unmounts).
  • Progress sub is aria-live="polite", tabular-nums; spinner + sweep respect prefers-reduced-motion; skeletons aria-hidden.
  • Skeleton mirrors each destination layout 1:1; real data swaps in row-by-row with no jump; prefers partial real content over full skeleton once anything lands.
  • Stats progressive: always render the 4 real summary cards (small or zero); no “come back in two weeks” wall; .stats-nudge below with importing / first-step copy; deltas “no prior period” on a fresh account.
  • Follower growth <2 days: “Growth starts tracking today”; header shows the real current count; never a flat 0-based line.
  • Follower count locked: “Follower insights unlock at ~100 followers”; lock glyph; count shows “—”; rest of page unaffected.
  • Viral tiers: “Calculating…” pill + per-row shimmer while the baseline forms; resolves to real counts/bars at ~5+ posts.
  • Tone warm + non-apologetic everywhere; no error styling on any first-run state.
  • Correct in light and dark; only semantic tokens (no hardcoded colour); copy survives all 8 locales without clipping.
