Pennedly · Mobile spec · per-screen

Calendar /app/calendar — phone

The place a creator sees and moves their upcoming timeline — manually scheduled drafts plus a projection of recurring autopilot posts, on the selected account. A 7-column week grid is too tight at 390px, so the phone’s default view is an Agenda: a vertical list grouped by day, with a compact week strip on top for navigation. Month is available as an option (a compact grid; tap a day → that day’s agenda). Each row is a chip in three honest variants — manual (solid, pencil), autopilot projection (ghost/dashed, clock, “text written at post time”), and failed (danger, with a reason + Retry). Tapping a row opens a bottom sheet (full-width 44px buttons, primary on top). Moving a post on a phone is done through Edit time — no drag. The Studio scheduling control (Publish now | Schedule) folds to a sheet too. Frames are the real component layer at 390px, light and dark. Calm calendar / clock / pencil iconography; no lightning bolt anywhere. Hours are UTC server-side, shown in the viewer’s local timezone (UTC+N).

What does not change. The data is the backend’s: manually scheduled drafts (source:'manual', with text) and the projected autopilot rules (source:'autopilot', text:null until post time). Statuses are scheduled / projected / failed. The Calendar only shows and moves — creation lives in the Studio, recurrence in Autopilot. Mobile is a layout pass: the week grid becomes an agenda; the popover becomes a sheet; drag becomes Edit time. Hours are UTC server-side, shown in the viewer’s local timezone.
New screen, built on our tokens. No third-party calendar library — the agenda, week strip, month grid, chips and sheets are all calendar.css on ds/tokens.css. The Autopilot screen is untouched; a projection’s sheet only links out to it. Calendar / clock / pencil icons only — no bolt.

4fixWeek strip + view toggle

A horizontal week strip sits under the top bar: seven 44px day buttons (Mon–Sun) with a small pip when that day has anything scheduled. The active/selected day is filled accent; today is the default selection. Below it, an Agenda | Month segmented toggle and the UTC+1 label.

Before

A 7-column week grid (the desktop view) at 390px crushes each column to ~50px — chips clip, snippets vanish, times collide.

After

The week becomes a navigation strip (tap a day to jump) and the body becomes a full-width agenda grouped by day — every chip gets the whole width, with a 2-line snippet and a ≥44px tap target.

Rules. The strip scrolls horizontally if the locale’s day labels run long; the active day is filled accent (its label/number flip to accent-foreground). Pips: filled = has posts, hollow = none. Tapping a day scrolls the agenda to that group (Month view: tapping a cell switches to that day’s agenda).

5newAgenda (default)

The body is a vertical list grouped by day. Each day-group has a header (“Today · Wed, Jun 17” + a count) and stacked rows. A row = the phone chip: a left time column (time + source glyph), the source label, and a 2-line snippet, with a status dot. Three variants, same honest coding as desktop: manual (solid, pencil, accent), projection (dashed/ghost, clock, italic “Drafted at post time”), failed (danger). Past rows dim to 50%.

Rules. Rows are sorted by time within a day. Manual rows carry the real snippet; projection rows show the italic placeholder (text is written at post time). Failed rows tint danger and surface a Retry in the sheet. The whole row is the tap target (≥44px) → opens the detail bottom-sheet.

6Month (option)

Month is available behind the toggle as a compact grid: seven narrow columns, day numbers, and up to three pips per cell (accent = manual, hollow = projection, danger = failed) — never text chips at this width. Today is an accent circle; other-month days dim. Tapping a cell switches back to Agenda focused on that day.

Why pips, not chips. At 390px a month cell is ~50px wide — too tight for even a time label. Pips give a faithful at-a-glance density; the agenda is where you read and act. Tapping any day is the bridge between the two.

7fixDetail bottom-sheet

Tapping a row opens a bottom sheet (our pattern: slide up from the bottom, rounded top, full-width 44px buttons, primary on top). Three variants mirror the desktop popover.

Before

A desktop popover anchored to a tiny chip is unreachable and clips off-screen on a phone.

After

A bottom sheet within thumb reach: the local time + UTC line, the text (or ghost placeholder), then stacked full-width actions — primary on top.

Rules. Manual → Edit time (primary) · Publish now · Unschedule · Delete. Projection → read-only; the only action is “Manage schedule” → Autopilot. Failed → a reason block + Retry (primary) · Delete. Moving a post is done through “Edit time” (a date + local-hour picker), never drag.

8newStudio scheduling

Part of the same feature: the Studio publish step. On a phone the publish dialog is a bottom sheet with a Publish now | Schedule segmented control. In Schedule mode it reveals full-width Date + Time fields (16px text, anti-zoom; local, with a “= HH:00 UTC” hint), a text preview + character count, and the primary button becomes “Schedule for Wed, Jun 18 · 09:00”. After scheduling, the draft card gets a “Scheduled · <date>” badge and moves to the Studio’s Scheduled filter.

Rules. Fields are 16px / ≥44px (iOS anti-zoom). Minimum offset is now + 5 min. The toast “Scheduled for <…>” carries an Undo. The badge and the Scheduled filter mirror the desktop Studio integration (Calendar-SPEC §4).

11Web ↔ mobile · Localization

Desktop ( > 880 )

  • Fixed sidebar; 960px column; Week/Month toggle + UTC label in the topbar.
  • Week = 7 columns; Month = 7×N grid with text chips + “+X more”.
  • Detail = popover anchored to the chip.
  • Move = drag & drop between columns + an Undo toast.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • No sidebar/tab bar; hamburger → drawer (Calendar active); free bottom edge.
  • Default = Agenda (day-grouped list) + a week strip; Month = compact pip grid.
  • Detail = bottom sheet (full-width buttons, primary on top).
  • Move = Edit time (date + local-hour picker), no drag.

All copy localizes into 8 locales (en/ru/uk/de/es/fr/it/pt); RU/DE run ~30–40% longer. Day-group headers, the Agenda/Month labels, source labels, sheet actions, the empty state and the Studio scheduling control all wrap (never clip). Day names/dates and times render in the viewer’s local timezone via a localized formatter.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — gutters stay 14px; the week strip scrolls horizontally if needed; agenda rows keep their 2-line snippet and ≥44px height; month pips stay legible; sheet buttons stay full-width. Long RU/DE day-group headers (“Сегодня · ср, 17 июн”) wrap to a second line rather than clip. The drawer stays min(82%, 312px) → ≈295px at 360.

12Checklist

  • Below 600px: no sidebar, no tab bar — one top bar with a hamburger far-left, “Calendar”, the UTC+1 label and a theme toggle; bottom edge free.
  • Hamburger opens the drawer (active = Calendar, calendar glyph, in the Workspace group right after Studio) + account at its foot.
  • Calendar / clock / pencil iconography; manual = pencil, projection = clock; no bolt anywhere.
  • Week strip: 44px day buttons Mon–Sun, pip when a day has posts, active day filled accent, today selected by default; scrolls on long locales.
  • Agenda (default): day-groups with header + count; rows sorted by time; three chip variants (manual solid / projection ghost-dashed / failed danger); past rows dim; ≥44px tap → sheet.
  • Month (option): compact grid with pips (accent / hollow / danger), today accent circle, other-month dim; tap a day → that day’s agenda.
  • Detail bottom sheet: local time + UTC line; manual (Edit time / Publish now / Unschedule / Delete) · projection (read-only + “Manage schedule” → Autopilot) · failed (reason + Retry); full-width buttons, primary on top.
  • Move = Edit time (date + local-hour picker), no drag on phone.
  • Studio sheet: Publish now | Schedule; Schedule reveals Date + Time (16px / ≥44px, “= HH:00 UTC”, ≥ +5 min) + preview + count; primary “Schedule for <…>”; toast w/ Undo; draft gets “Scheduled · <date>” badge + Scheduled filter.
  • Empty = “Nothing scheduled yet” (+ autopilot note + CTA); Loading = skeleton mirroring the agenda; correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour.
  • All copy survives the longest of 8 locales — never clipped; sanity-checked at 360px; times in the viewer’s local timezone.
