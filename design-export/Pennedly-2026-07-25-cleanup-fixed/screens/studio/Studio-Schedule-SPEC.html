Pennedly · Spec · Studio — publish-later (scheduling)

Scheduling /app — make “schedule for later” discoverable

Today the only way to find scheduling is to press Publish, then spot a “Publish now / Schedule” switch inside the dialog — you can’t tell scheduling exists until you’ve already committed to publishing. This spec moves the entry point onto the ready draft card: a labelled Schedule button beside Publish (finalized from the reviewed options). Tapping it lands in the confirm dialog, opened in the right mode. It also documents the dialog’s Publish now / Schedule modes (Date + Time + a UTC echo, ≥ now + 5 min), the “Scheduled” card state, and the Scheduled tab. Reschedule / unschedule / retry live on the Calendar — Studio links out. Every state is the real card + dialog (studio.css + calendar.css + studio-schedule.css), light and dark.

Extend, don’t redesign. The dialog, its account preview, char-meter and the Publish now / Schedule control are the shipped controls (specced in the Calendar spec). The only new thing is the card-level entry point. Publish-now stays one tap — surfacing scheduling can’t slow the common case. Scheduling requires ≥ now + 5 min and always echoes the local time as UTC. No new icon is needed.

0The problem & the ask

Scheduling is invisible until you’ve pressed Publish. The fix: make “Schedule” visible on the ready card itself, without pushing Publish out of the one-tap common case. Below, the same ready card with each proposed entry point.

Today — hidden

The ready card foot is just Publish. Scheduling only appears after you open the dialog and find the “Publish now / Schedule” switch — you can’t tell it exists.

Proposed — visible on the card

A scheduling affordance sits on the ready card foot beside Publish, so “later” is discoverable up front. The dialog then opens straight into the matching mode.

1Entry point on the card

The finalized entry on the ready card foot: a labelled Schedule (clock) beside the primary Publish, with the ⋯ overflow. “Schedule for later” is visible up front — the discovery failure is fixed — while Publish stays the one-tap common case. Tapping Schedule opens the dialog in Schedule mode; Publish opens it in Publish-now mode.

Why two labelled buttons. Of the reviewed options, this is the most discoverable — “Schedule” is a visible, named action rather than hidden behind a caret or an ambiguous icon (which is the very failure we’re fixing). Publish keeps its primary weight, so nothing about the common case slows down.
Light frame Dark frame Each frame is the real ready card at the feed-column width

2Dialog · Publish now

The confirm dialog, opened in Publish now mode (the default, and where the “Publish” entry lands). Account preview + post text + char-meter; the Publish now / Schedule segmented control sits at the top as an escape hatch. Primary: Publish now (IcCheck).

3Dialog · Schedule (empty)

Opened in Schedule mode (where the “Schedule” entry lands): empty Date + Time, a hint to pick a time ≥ 5 min out, and the Schedule primary disabled until a valid time is set.

4Dialog · Schedule (valid)

Date + Time filled with a time ≥ 5 min out. The hint echoes the chosen local time as UTC — “= 14:30 UTC · at least 5 min out” — and the primary becomes “Schedule for Sat, Jun 16 · 14:30” (IcClock), enabled.

5Dialog · Schedule (too soon)

A time less than 5 minutes out: the hint turns danger and the Schedule primary stays disabled — the picked Date/Time persist so the user only nudges the time.

6Card · Scheduled

Once scheduled, the draft gets an accent “Scheduled” badge and its foot reads 🕐 “Goes out Sat, Jun 16, 14:30” (local). In Studio this is mostly read-only — the ⋯-menu and a “Manage on Calendar” link route to the Calendar, where reschedule / unschedule / publish-now / retry live.

7Tab · Scheduled (active)

A scheduled draft is a status between ready and published: it moves to a “Scheduled” tab (accent dot) in the Studio status filter.

8Tab · Scheduled (empty)

The empty-state for the Scheduled tab when nothing is queued — with a way into the Calendar.

✓Checklist

  • Scheduling is discoverable on the ready card itself — a labelled Schedule button beside Publish.
  • Publish stays the one-tap primary; the common case isn’t slowed.
  • Dialog · Publish now: account + text + char-meter + the now/schedule control; primary “Publish now”.
  • Dialog · Schedule empty: empty Date/Time, ≥5-min hint, Schedule disabled.
  • Dialog · Schedule valid: “= 14:30 UTC · at least 5 min out”; primary “Schedule for …” enabled.
  • Dialog · Schedule too soon: danger hint; Schedule disabled; picked time persists.
  • Card · Scheduled: accent “Scheduled” badge + “Goes out …” foot + ⋯/Calendar link (read-only in Studio).
  • Tab · Scheduled between Ready and Published (accent dot); + a per-tab empty state.
  • Reschedule / unschedule / retry are linked out to the Calendar, not rebuilt here.
  • ≥ now + 5 min enforced; local time always echoed as UTC; numbers tabular; labels wrap on long locales.
  • Correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour; no new icon.
