Pennedly · Mobile spec · Studio — Ideas hook-brainstorm

Ideas /app — phone

The phone version of the Ideas panel. The shipped action-row chips already scroll horizontally with a right edge-fade; the “✨ Ideas” pill leads that scroller. Tapping it brainstorms ~8 hooks in the account’s voice into a full-width panel — and the open question this spec answers is bottom-sheet vs inline. The recommendation is inline (§2): it keeps the ideas physically attached to the composer they seed. Idea cards are ≥44px and comfortably tappable; the header refresh/close are ≥44px targets; inputs stay ≥16px. Same states, tokens, flows and copy as the desktop spec — only the layout adapts below 600px. Every frame is the real component layer at 390px (sanity-checked at 360), light and dark.

What doesn’t change. The brief → generate flow, the suggestion chips, the count select + Generate, the 500-char limit, light + dark — all identical to desktop and to the shipped mobile composer. Ideas are ephemeral (never saved) and free (no quota/cost UI). On pick, the hook replaces the editor text (seeds the brief). This is a layout pass on one new surface — the composer itself is not restyled.

1Identity & placement

The Ideas pill is the first chip in the composer’s horizontal chip row. The panel renders inline under the action row — pushing the sticky filter row and feed down — exactly where the shipped feature placed it. Here it is in full context: top bar, composer with the panel open, then the filter row and a draft card below.

2decisionInline, not a bottom-sheet

The brief asks us to choose: does the panel become a bottom-sheet on phones, or stay inline? Recommendation: stay inline, matching desktop.

Bottom-sheet — rejected

Detaches the ideas from the composer they seed, so the “tap → it flows up into the editor” cause-and-effect is lost. A modal also over-weights a casual, repeatable, free action and adds a scrim + dismiss the inline version doesn’t need.

Inline — chosen

Keeps the panel attached to the editor right above it, so a tapped hook visibly seeds the brief. Lighter, quieter, and it reuses the shipped placement. Cost: it pushes the feed down — mitigated below.

Mitigations for the inline trade-off. On open, the panel scrolls into view under the composer. The idea list grows with content (the screen scrolls — no nested scroll trap), and closing or picking collapses it entirely. The chip row keeps its right edge-fade; the panel is full-width within the composer’s 14px gutter.

3The chip in the scroller

The pill keeps its accent tint inside the horizontally-scrolling chip row (full-size chips, right edge-fade). Disabled (no account / no active voice) it dims; the hint rides the same scroller as a one-line note rather than wrapping.

4Loading

Pressed chip (sparkle pulses, disabled) opens the panel: “Brainstorming ideas in your voice…” over shimmer placeholders. The pulse + shimmer honor prefers-reduced-motion.

5Results — the panel

Full-width panel: a header (caption + 44px refresh + 44px close) over the vertical list of idea cards. Each card is a bold hook + a muted angle, comfortably ≥44px tall.

6Idea card → tap → seed

The whole card is the tap target; the “Use ↑” pill stays visible on touch (no hover). Tapping replaces the editor text with that hook and closes the panel — the brief is seeded, Generate enables, and a small accent note confirms it.

7Error & empty

Both quiet. Error (503): “Couldn’t get ideas.” + a ≥44px “Try again”. Empty (422, no voice/posts yet): “No ideas right now.” + a one-line nudge. No red banner — brainstorming is free and repeatable.

8Localization & 360px

All copy localizes into 8 locales; DE/RU/UK run ~30–40% longer. The chip label and caption never clip; hooks + angles wrap to as many lines as they need. At 360px the same rules hold — the composer just gets ~30px narrower and the chip row absorbs it by scrolling.

＊Icon refresh — three primary actions

New line-icons for Studio’s three primary actions, same calm family, shown on the real 44px mobile buttons in light and dark. Each is named for icons.tsx; full before→after rationale is in the desktop spec.

Keep the set coherent. IcNib → IcGenerate (text-lines + pen) on Generate; IcCheck → IcApprove (check-in-circle) on Approve; IcStudio → IcPublish (paper-plane) on Publish to Threads. The publish dialog’s “Publish now” confirm — currently IcCheck — switches to IcPublish to match. IcCheck stays the generic save mark; IcSend stays the tweak-bar “send” affordance (distinct from the plane).
