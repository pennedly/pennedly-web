Pennedly · Mobile spec · per-screen

Autopilot /app/autopilot — phone

Opt-in automation a creator assembles from a few clear controls — off by default, framed as “you stay in control”, never “Threads on autopilot”. On a phone the controls are what break: the master card, the schedule object grids, the expanded reply policy, and the reworked Activity all need to fold cleanly at 390px (sanity-checked at 360). This spec folds them: every select/input is 16px text + ≥44px tap, PolicyRows drop their control full-width below the label, object field grids go one column, the confirm dialog becomes a bottom sheet (primary on top), and the Activity all-time header wraps to two stat lines. Read-only where the desktop is; same tokens. Shell = hamburger + slide-in drawer, no tab bar, free bottom edge. Frames are the real component layer at 390px, light and dark. Clock iconography throughout; auto-replies use a chat bubble; no lightning bolt anywhere.

pennedly-mobile.css autopilot-app.jsx autopilot-parts.jsx shell-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
What does not change. OFF for everyone by default; the master gate; the account-level reply policy (audience · adds-value · frequency · daily cap · quiet hours); schedule objects (local hour stored as UTC, jitter, topic, seed); and read-only Activity are all from the backend. Mobile is a layout pass; the only structural change is the shared app shell. Hours are UTC server-side, shown/picked in the viewer’s local timezone.
Specifies the updated screen. The live screen diverged from the old simpler concept: it is now a rule-based scheduler + an expanded account-level reply policy with a reworked, read-only Activity. Clock icons replace the old bolt; auto-replies use a chat bubble. Reconcile to this, not the older autopilot-app.jsx.

4fixMaster switch + confirm

The global on/off. The card keeps the toggle on the right with the title wrapping; the sub-line reads “On · posting and replying on your schedule” / “Off · you approve everything yourself”. When off, a reassurance block sits below. Turning it on opens a confirm — on a phone the dialog becomes a bottom sheet with the primary action on top.

Before

A centered desktop modal feels stranded on a phone; the master row’s big switch + long title collide.

After

The card header keeps the toggle right while the title/sub wrap; the confirm slides up as a bottom sheet — “Turn on autopilot” primary on top, “Cancel” below — within thumb reach.

Rules. Clock glyph in the master tile and the sheet. Toggle is the big variant (54×30). Flipping on → sheet; flipping off is immediate (toast). The sheet’s primary is full-width on top; cancel is a secondary full-width below.

5fixScheduled posts

Each schedule is a card: an editable name (“#id” fallback), an enable/Paused toggle, delete, then three fields — Post time (a local hour picker showing the live UTC±N + “sends HH:00 UTC”), Random spread (Exact / ± N min), Topic (any round-robin / a saved topic) — plus a per-object seed “New posts from this schedule start with auto-reply on”. Object count = posts/day.

Before

The desktop 3-up field grid (time / spread / topic) crushes the selects below 44px and the hints clip.

After

The grid folds to one column; each select is 16px text / ≥44px tall with its hint below; the card actions stack. Delete is an inline confirm (“Delete this object?” · Cancel / Delete).

Local ↔ UTC. The hour picker is whole-hour, local; the hint shows UTC±N · sends HH:00 UTC. The canonical value is UTC post_hour — converted via the browser timezone, nothing persisted on conversion. Empty state: “No scheduled posts yet” + “Add a schedule”.

6newAuto-reply policy (reply mode)

At the top of the card, a full-width 3-segment control — Off / All posts / Selected — one choice of three (the active segment is raised). Off dims the rest of the policy (50%, non-interactive); Selected shows a hint that auto-replies go only to posts flagged in My Feed. Below, the body is a vertical stack of PolicyRows; the control drops full-width below its label.

Rules. Three equal segments, ≥44px each; labels wrap on long locales (DE/RU) rather than clip. “Off” visibly disables the audience / frequency / cap / quiet rows so it’s clear nothing auto-replies. “Selected” is populated by the per-post flag on My Feed.

7newActivity (reworked)

Read-only. A Posts / Replies tab toggle, then an all-time summary header — “All time — N posts · M auto-replies” (account-wide). Below it, per-object cards showing each schedule’s all-time post count + “last <date>” — posts only (auto-replies are account-level, summed in the header, not per-object). Then the tab body: Posts (object tag · date · text · views/likes/comments · Threads link) or Replies (the commenter’s comment + the bot’s reply + a link).

Why replies are account-level. Auto-replies answer comments on any post, so they aren’t tied to one schedule — they’re summed once in the all-time header. The old per-object reply counter (which always read 0) is gone. On a phone the all-time header wraps to two stat lines; per-object cards stack; list cards are full-width. Empty: “No activity yet”.

10Web ↔ mobile

Desktop ( > 880 )

  • Fixed sidebar; 960px column; master pill in topbar.
  • Object: 3-up fields (time/spread/topic) in a row; actions in the head.
  • PolicyRow: label left, control right.
  • Activity: all-time line + 3-up per-object cards; list full-width.
  • Confirm = centered modal dialog.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • No sidebar/tab bar; hamburger → drawer + account; free bottom edge.
  • Object: fields 1 column; actions stack; inputs 16px / ≥44px.
  • PolicyRow: control full-width below label; quiet From/to wraps.
  • Activity: all-time header wraps to 2 stat lines; per-object cards stack; list full-width.
  • Confirm = bottom sheet (primary on top).

11Localization & 360px

Master/sub copy, the confirm, all five policy labels + helpers, the audience/frequency/cap options, the quiet-hours labels, the Activity captions and tags all localize into 8 locales (en/ru/uk/de/es/fr/it/pt); RU/DE run ~30–40% longer. On a phone: policy labels wrap above their full-width control (never clipped); the quiet From/to row wraps; the all-time header wraps to two stat lines; object hints wrap under their select. Clock times and timestamps render in the viewer’s local timezone.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — the gutter stays 14px; object fields and per-object cards are already one column; PolicyRow controls are full-width; the quiet From/to selects wrap to their own line; the all-time header keeps its two stat lines. Long RU/DE policy labels (“Отвечать, только когда это уместно”) wrap to 2–3 lines and the selects hold 16px / ≥44px. The confirm sheet stays full-width with the primary on top. The drawer stays min(82%, 312px) → ≈295px at 360.

12Checklist

  • Below 600px: no sidebar, no tab bar — one top bar with a hamburger far-left and (when on) the “Active” success pill with a live dot; bottom edge free.
  • Hamburger opens the drawer (active = Autopilot, clock glyph, tester-gated) + account at its foot.
  • Clock iconography on master / post tags / confirm / nav; auto-replies use a chat bubble; no bolt anywhere.
  • Master card: toggle right, title/sub wrap; “On · posting and replying on your schedule” / “Off · you approve everything yourself”; OFF default; off → reassurance.
  • Turning on opens a bottom sheet (“Turn on autopilot” primary on top, Cancel below); off is immediate.
  • Scheduled posts: 1-column fields; Post time (local hour + UTC±N + sends HH:00 UTC); Random spread (Exact / ±N); Topic (any round-robin / saved); seed; inline delete-confirm; ≥44px / 16px controls; empty = “No scheduled posts yet”.
  • Auto-reply policy: master switch; body dims when off; 5 rows in order with the control full-width below the label; adds-value is the weighted hero (default on); quiet hours reveals a wrapping From/to local row + UTC±N; cap 10/25/50 (legacy selectable).
  • Activity: Posts/Replies tabs; all-time header wraps to two stat lines (N posts · M auto-replies, account-wide); per-object posts-only cards (count + last date); Posts/Replies lists full-width; empty = “No activity yet”.
  • Footer: “follows your Voice and Style rules” + safety line.
  • Loading = skeleton mirroring the layout; correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour.
  • All copy survives the longest of 8 locales — never clipped; sanity-checked at 360px.
