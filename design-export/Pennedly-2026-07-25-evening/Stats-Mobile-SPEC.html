Stats /app/stats — phone

Account-level analytics on a phone. Synced to three real product changes: the trend chart is now an area-line (not bars); "best times" is one heatmap (not two bar charts); the topbar has a working Refresh control with a relative "updated N ago" caption. .stats-empty is gone — a fresh account gets the progressive first-run treatment (real small/zero cards + a warm nudge), not a wall; see First-Run-SPEC.html. Read-only otherwise, same tokens and computed numbers as the desktop spec; only the layout changes below 600px, on the decided shell (hamburger + drawer, no tab bar, free bottom edge).

pennedly-mobile.css stats-app.jsx stats-firstrun.css shell-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
Synced to the current product. Trend chart: bars → area-line with a dashed "avg" reference and gap-filled buckets. Best times: two bar charts → one 7×6 heatmap (day × hour-of-day), legend "less"/"more", empty cells shown as a distinct dashed state (not the dimmest heat colour). Topbar: static "Updated daily" pill → working Refresh button + "updated N ago" caption. .stats-empty removed → progressive first-run page; period selector hidden (not disabled) during loading and first-run.
What does not change. The 6 fixed backend periods, the computed summary totals + "vs previous period" deltas, the "× your average" verdicts, the best-slot rule (≥2 posts), and the local-timezone timing data are all from the backend — read-only, no per-post drilldown beyond the link out. Mobile is a layout pass; the only structural change is the shared app shell.

4fixPeriod + summary fold

The period selector becomes a horizontally scrollable segmented row (all 6 reachable, ≥44px tap, right-edge mask fade) — and is hidden entirely during loading and first-run, not shown disabled. The summary stacks: hero Views at text-h1, then a 3-up row (Posts/Likes/Comments) at text-h3.

5fixThe trend chart — area-line

Was a bar chart; now a continuous line with a light fill under it, same gap-filled axis (every bucket present; empty buckets show as a small muted dot on the zero line, not a break). A dashed reference line marks the average of posted buckets, labelled "avg". The SVG uses preserveAspectRatio="none" so it always fills the panel width — no separate "many buckets" scroll mode is needed on the phone.

Rules. Plot height 140px; line + fill scale to width regardless of bucket count. Granularity is per period: hourly (Today/Yesterday) · daily (7 days/Month) · weekly (3 months/All time), viewer's local time. Sparse labels (~8) sit below, live DOM text.

6Follower growth

Unchanged: a min–max-scaled line (never 0-based). Header: current count + gain over the window. Ignores the period — full history since connect. Below 2 days of data: a "collecting" tile.

7Top posts

Unchanged: top-5 by views, rank + 2-line snippet + meta + "N× your average" verdict, colour-graded. Whole row taps to Threads.

8fixBest times to post — heatmap

Was two stacked bar charts (By hour, By day of week); now one heatmap: 7 day rows × 6 hour-of-day columns, colour = avg views/post for that slot from 4 heat levels (--color-heat-1…4). A legend reads "less → more"; the best slot (≥2 posts) is outlined and named in a "Best · …" chip. Empty slots (0 posts) get a distinct dashed cell, not the dimmest heat colour — "no posts" and "few views" must not look the same. Hidden entirely for Today/Yesterday.

Phone sizing. Row labels narrow to ~26px; all 6 hour columns stay visible without horizontal scroll (unlike the old per-chart bar width problem). Local timezone throughout.

9Performance spread

Unchanged: four viral tiers (Viral/Good/Average/Weak), colour dot + name + count + percent + proportion bar, stacked full-width.

10States

12Web ↔ mobile

Desktop ( > 900 )

  • Fixed sidebar; 960px column; Refresh + "updated N ago" in the topbar.
  • Period selector in one row (hidden in loading/first-run); summary as a 4-up grid.
  • Heatmap: 7×6 grid, all cells visible, no scroll.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • No sidebar/tab bar; hamburger → drawer + account; free bottom edge.
  • Period selector scrolls (hidden in loading/first-run); summary stacks.
  • Line chart and heatmap both fit the narrower panel width directly — no separate scroll mode.

13Localization & 360px

Period labels, "avg", the "× your average" verdict, the heatmap legend ("less"/"more") and "Best · …" chip, tier names, and "updated N ago" all localize into 8 locales; DE/RU/UK run ~30–40% longer. Bucket labels are live DOM text, never baked into the chart image.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — gutters stay 14px; the heatmap keeps its 6 columns (row labels narrow further); the line chart simply scales; period row already scrolls.

14Checklist

  • Topbar has a working Refresh (spins while fetching) + "updated N ago" caption — not a static pill.
  • Period selector hidden entirely (not disabled) during loading and first-run.
  • Trend is an area-line (line + fill + dashed "avg"), gap-filled, always fills panel width.
  • Best times is one heatmap (7×6), legend "less"/"more", empty cells visually distinct from low-value cells; best slot (≥2 posts) outlined + chip.
  • .stats-empty is gone — first-run shows real small/zero summary cards + a nudge, never a wall.
  • Follower growth, top posts, and performance spread panels unchanged in logic.
  • Panel order: period → summary → followers → trend → top posts → best times → spread.
  • Correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour; sanity-checked at 360px.
