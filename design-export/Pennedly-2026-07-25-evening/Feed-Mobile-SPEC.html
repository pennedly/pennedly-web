Pennedly · Mobile spec · per-screen

My Feed /app/feed — phone

Your published Threads posts with inline analytics — read-only, each post measured against your own average. On a phone the desktop content could overlap: the per-card metric grid and the growth chart are the parts that need folding. This spec fits them cleanly at 390px (sanity-checked at 360) on the decided mobile shell — a slim top bar with a hamburger that opens a left navigation drawer, no bottom tab bar, free bottom edge. Same tokens, metrics, virality logic and states as the desktop spec — only the layout changes below 600px. Every frame is the real component layer at 390px, light and dark.

pennedly-mobile.css feed-app.jsx feed-card.jsx shell-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
Synced to the current product: the Baseline summary card is gone. The feed used to open with a 4-up stat card (avg views/likes/comments/reposts + sparkline) above the sort bar. That block was removed (desktop commit fb3e0b2) — it is not moved, hidden, or replaced. The feed now opens directly with the sort bar, then post cards. The per-card ratio badge (“N× average”) is unchanged and still the only place that number shows.
What does not change. The average-relative virality bands, the views/likes/comments/reposts metric set, the cumulative growth chart vs your average, the auto-replies toggle, translate-on-every-card, tester-gated Delete, reply-context, and the loading/empty/error states are all identical to desktop. Feed is read-only analytics — no post editing here. Mobile is a layout pass; the only structural change is the shared app shell (tab bar → hamburger drawer).

2The phone layout

The whole screen in context, light and dark: the sort bar directly under the title, then post cards each carrying metrics, a virality badge, the auto-replies pill and the two-tier foot. The feed runs to the free bottom edge.

3shellShell: top bar + drawer

The shell is shared by every screen (see DS · App shell & nav) — one slim 52px bar; the hamburger slides the drawer in from the left. Feed-specific shell values:

SlotFeed value
HamburgerFar left, opens .m-drawer (active row = My Feed).

Freshness pillstatus-pill--success with a clock — “Updated hourly”. Reflects that metrics refresh periodically. Drops below the title row only if a locale is very long.

Drawer. Identical to every screen: brand header, grouped nav with live badges, active row gets a left accent rail (here = My Feed), account control pinned at the foot. Width min(82%, 312px); scrim or ✕ dismiss. Nothing about nav is invented per-screen.

4Sort bar

With the Baseline card removed, this is the first thing under the title on every screen size — a post count plus a Recent / Top segmented control. Nothing to fold here: it was never the crowded part.

Rules. The segmented control is Recent / Top (shortened from “Top performing” to fit; the full label localizes and the control wraps to its own line before it would clip). The count reads “{n} published posts”, tabular-nums.

5Metrics, virality & growth

The metric block goes vertical: the hero (views) on its own line, the three secondary metrics in an even row below (they wrap on long locales). The virality badge stays top-right of the header; “Growth” expands the cumulative chart inline, drawn against your-average. Four virality bands judge each post against your account average:

BandMeaning · phone treatment
over (≥1.5×)Success-tinted “N× average” with an up arrow. Strong post.
on par (0.85–1.5×)Neutral “On par”.
under (<0.85×)Neutral “N× average” (e.g. 0.6×). No alarm colour — this is intentional, not an error tone.
still settlingWarning-tinted clock “Still settling” on very fresh posts whose metrics aren’t stable yet; the growth caption reads “Still climbing”.
Growth panelFull-width viewBox SVG; dashed “your average” reference line + last-point marker. The Growth icon button reads “on” while expanded.

6fixPost-card foot — the fix

Before

“Auto-replies · Growth · Open on Threads · ⋯” share one row. On a phone it crams: “Open on Threads” wraps to two lines and the ⋯ overflows off the right edge.

After

The auto-replies pill moves to its own meta line. The action row keeps one primary — Open on Threads (grows) — with Growth collapsed to a 44px icon and ⋯ as a 44px overflow holding Translate / Open / Delete.

The ⋯ is right-anchored (last in the row), so it opens leftward fully inside the frame. It holds the secondary actions — Delete is tester-gated:

Overflow contents (⋯). Show original (if translated) · Translate ▸ · Open on Threads · — · Delete post (danger, tester-only). Identical to desktop; only the trigger collapses to 44×44.

7Card details

The details that ride on a card, each fitting the phone width: the auto-replies pill state, a translated body, a reply post’s context block, and the “still settling” fresh-post treatment.

Auto-reply flag (per-post). The pill is the override that populates Autopilot’s “Selected” reply mode — All = every post on by default, Selected = only flagged posts, Off = none. Toggling fires a toast: “Auto-replies on/off for this post” + a sub line (“Pennedly will draft replies to new comments” / “New comments won’t get drafted replies”).
DetailPhone behavior
auto-repliesOwn meta line; ink border + reply icon when on, hollow dot when off. Toggles in place.
translatedBody swaps to the translation; a .translate-bar reads “Translated to {native} · Show original”. Each post’s own language is the original.
reply postA quoted reply-context block above the body; “replied to @who” in the sub-line, wraps.
still settlingWarning clock badge; small early metrics; “Still climbing” growth caption.

10Web ↔ mobile

Desktop ( > 900 )

  • Fixed sidebar; 960px column; topbar with freshness pill.
  • Sort + count on one line, directly under the title; metric row inline.
  • Foot is one row (pill + Growth + Open + ⋯).
  • Delete confirm = centered modal dialog.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • No sidebar/tab bar; hamburger → left drawer + account; free bottom edge.
  • Sort bar is its own row, directly under the title; metric block vertical (hero + subs).
  • Foot is two-tier (pill line + grow-primary + 44px Growth/⋯).
  • Delete confirm = bottom sheet, danger primary on top.

11Localization & 360px

“Open on Threads”, “Auto-replies on/off”, the virality phrases, sort labels and metric labels all localize into 8 locales; DE/RU/UK run ~30–40% longer. On a phone: the primary wraps rather than clips; the auto-replies pill and virality badge wrap before truncating; the sort segmented control wraps to its own line; metric labels (“views”) stay beside the number and wrap the row if a locale runs long. Never bake a metric or label into the chart image — the SVG text uses live <text>.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — the gutter stays 14px and the sort bar and post cards just get ~30px narrower. Metric subs and the segmented control already wrap; the growth SVG is width-100% so it simply scales. Long primary labels are a touch more likely to wrap to two lines (still ≥44px). The drawer stays min(82%, 312px) → 82% (≈295px) at 360.

12Checklist

  • Below 600px there is no sidebar and no tab bar — one 52px top bar with a hamburger far-left and the “Updated hourly” pill.
  • Hamburger opens the left drawer (active = My Feed) + account at its foot; bottom edge free.
  • No Baseline summary card above the sort bar, at any width — the feed opens with sort, then cards.
  • Sort bar is its own row directly under the title (Recent / Top), count tabular.
  • Metric block is vertical: hero views line + secondary row; numbers tabular.
  • All four virality bands present; over = success, settling = warning clock, on-par/under = neutral (no alarm colour); each wraps before clipping.
  • Growth chart is a full-width SVG with the live “your average” line; Growth icon reads “on” when expanded.
  • Post-card foot is two-tier: auto-replies pill on its own line; one grow-primary; Growth + ⋯ at 44×44.
  • “Open on Threads” wraps on long locales — never clipped, ⋯ never off-edge; ⋯ opens fully inside the frame.
  • ⋯ holds Translate / Open / Delete (Delete tester-gated, danger); translated shows “Show original”.
  • Reply posts show the quoted-comment context; “replied to @who” wraps.
  • Loading = 5 skeletons (sort bar hidden); empty is a friendly state (sort bar hidden); error banner has Retry; Delete confirms in a bottom sheet.
  • Correct in light and dark; sanity-checked at 360px; no hardcoded colour.
