Pattern study /app/patterns — phone

A self-study: Pennedly reads back through your own published posts and surfaces the patterns that correlate with more engagement — “posts that open with a question”, “short posts”, “posts about revision” — each with a ×N multiplier for how much better it performs vs your baseline, the evidence, and example posts of yours. (The separate “Explore patterns” screen studies broader content — not this one.) A reading-width, read-only insight screen. Same tokens, findings and states as the desktop spec; only the layout changes below 600px, on the decided shell (hamburger + drawer, no tab bar, free bottom edge). Frames are the real component layer at 390px, light and dark.

pennedly-mobile.css patterns-app.jsx patterns-parts.jsx shell-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
What does not change. Pattern study is read-only insight, computed from the user’s own published posts; it needs a minimum amount of content before it can find patterns (hence the not-enough-data state). The ×N is derived from the engagement delta of posts with the trait, backed by the evidence comparison and real example posts. No user actions beyond reading; no patterns the backend doesn’t produce. (The earlier “Topics” chip stays dropped.) Mobile is a layout pass; the only structural change is the shared app shell.

4fixPattern card — the fix

Each pattern card carries a lot: the trait, the ×N, the lifted metric, an evidence comparison (lead vs baseline), and example posts. On desktop the evidence sits as label · bar · value on one line. That triple doesn’t fit 390px.

Before

The evidence row [label | bar | value] crushes: the bar shrinks to a stub and the value wraps under the label. The ×N competes with the headline for the same line.

After

Everything stacks. The ×N stays a big, prominent number beside the wrapping headline + lifted metric. Each evidence row becomes label + value on top of a full-width track bar (lead = accent, baseline = muted). Example posts are full-width inset quotes with their metric below.

Rules. The ×N is 2.15rem/700 in accent — the most prominent thing on the card, never shrunk. Evidence bars are scaled to the larger of the two values; the “n=” sample sizes fold into the note line. Examples wrap freely (no clamp — reading them is the point); if a card grows very tall, the examples are the natural disclosure boundary. The lifted-metric line (“more comments”) reads in success ink.

5Strength & long examples

A pattern is either a strong signal (success) or worth testing (warning, fewer posts behind it). Example posts vary in length — a long one just grows the card and wraps, never truncates.

8Web ↔ mobile

Desktop ( > 900 )

  • Fixed sidebar; ~720px reading column.
  • Evidence rows as label · bar · value on one line.
  • ×N beside the headline; examples inset.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • No sidebar/tab bar; hamburger → drawer + account; free bottom edge.
  • Evidence rows stack: label+value over a full-width bar.
  • ×N stays prominent beside the wrapping headline; examples full-width.

9Localization & 360px

Headlines, kind tags, strength labels, evidence labels and the lifted-metric phrase all localize into 8 locales; DE/RU/UK run ~30–40% longer. On a phone: the ×N is locale-neutral and stays prominent; the headline wraps beside it; evidence labels wrap above their bar (the value stays on the right); example posts wrap freely; the strength badge wraps under the kind tag rather than clipping. Numbers are tabular and locale-formatted.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — the gutter stays 14px; cards get ~30px narrower so headlines and examples rewrap to more lines and evidence bars get a touch shorter. The ×N never shrinks. The drawer stays min(82%, 312px) → 82% (≈295px) at 360.

10Checklist

  • Below 600px there is no sidebar and no tab bar — one 52px top bar with a hamburger far-left; “Pattern study” truncates if needed.
  • Hamburger opens the left drawer (active = Pattern study) + account; bottom edge free.
  • Intro frames it as the user’s OWN posts; no “Topics” chip.
  • Each card: kind tag + strength (strong/worth-testing · N posts), a prominent ×N, the lifted metric, evidence comparison, example posts.
  • ×N stays big and legible (accent, 2.15rem) — never shrunk to fit.
  • Evidence rows stack (label+value over a full-width bar; lead accent vs muted baseline).
  • Example posts wrap freely; a long example just grows the card — no clipping.
  • Loading = skeleton cards; not-enough-data shows a progress bar toward the minimum; error banner has Retry.
  • Read-only — no accept/reject, no editing.
  • Labels wrap on long locales — never clipped; sanity-checked at 360px.
  • Correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour.
