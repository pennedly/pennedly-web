My Feed /app/feed — phone

Your published posts, each read against your average. Same content, metrics, virality logic and states as the desktop spec — only the layout changes below 600px. Feed is where the card action-row problem bit hardest (Auto-replies / Growth / Open in Threads / ⋯ all in one row), so it’s the clearest demonstration of the shared two-tier foot. Frames are the real component layer at 390px, light and dark.

pennedly-mobile.css feed-app.jsx feed-card.jsx shell-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
What does not change. The baseline-relative virality badge, the views/likes/comments/reposts metric set, the cumulative growth chart vs your average, auto-replies toggle, translate, delete, and the loading/empty/error states are all identical to desktop. Mobile is a layout pass only.

4fixPost-card foot — the fix

Before

“Auto-replies · Growth · Open in Threads · ⋯” share one row. On a phone it crams: “Open in Threads” wraps to two lines and the ⋯ overflows off the right edge.

After

The auto-replies pill moves to its own meta line. The action row keeps one primary — Open on Threads (grows) — with Growth collapsed to a 44px icon and ⋯ as a 44px overflow holding Translate / Open / Delete.

Overflow contents (⋯). Show original (if translated) · Translate ▸ · Open on Threads · — · Delete post (danger). Identical to desktop; only the trigger collapses to 44×44.

5Metrics, virality & growth

The metric block goes vertical: the hero (views) on its own line, the three secondary metrics in an even row below (they wrap on long locales). The virality badge stays top-right of the header; “Growth” expands the cumulative chart inline, drawn against your-average.

9Web ↔ mobile

Desktop ( > 900 )

  • 960px column; average-baseline summary at the top.
  • Metric row inline; foot is one row (pill + Growth + Open + ⋯).
  • Growth chart inline on expand.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • Edge-to-edge cards; baseline folds into each chart label.
  • Metric block vertical; foot is two-tier (pill line + action row).
  • Open on Threads is the grow-primary; Growth + ⋯ are 44px icons.

10Localization

“Open on Threads”, “Auto-replies on/off”, the virality phrases and metric labels all localize. On a phone: the primary wraps rather than clips; the auto-replies pill and virality badge wrap before truncating; metric labels (“views”) stay beside the number and wrap the row if a locale runs long. Never bake a metric or label into the chart image — the SVG text uses live <text>.

11Checklist

  • Post-card foot is two-tier: auto-replies pill on its own line; one grow-primary; Growth + ⋯ at 44×44.
  • “Open on Threads” wraps on long locales — never clipped, ⋯ never off-edge.
  • Metric block is vertical: hero line + secondary row; numbers tabular.
  • Virality badge sits top-right and wraps before truncating.
  • Growth chart is full-width SVG with live “your average” label.
  • Reply posts show the quoted-comment context; “replied to @who” wraps.
  • Loading = 3 skeletons; empty + error reachable; Retry present.
  • Correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour.
