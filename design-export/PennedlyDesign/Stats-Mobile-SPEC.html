Stats /app/stats — phone

Account-level analytics on a phone: the headline numbers, what worked, when to post, and how viral posts were — over a chosen period. Since the last pass the screen grew two panels (Top posts, Best times to post) and the trend chart was rebuilt into a continuous, gap-filled axis. This spec fits all of it cleanly at 390px (sanity-checked at 360): a horizontally-scrollable period selector, a stacked summary (Views hero + 3-up), a bar chart that fills for short windows and scrolls sideways for long ones, top-5 posts, the by-hour / by-day timing charts, and the viral-tier spread. Read-only, same tokens and computed numbers as the desktop spec; only the layout changes below 600px, on the decided shell (hamburger + drawer, no tab bar, free bottom edge). Frames are the real component layer at 390px, light and dark.

pennedly-mobile.css stats-app.jsx stats-parts.jsx shell-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
What does not change. The 6 fixed backend periods (Today / Yesterday / 7 days / Month / 3 months / All time), the computed summary totals + “vs previous period” deltas, the gap-filled average-views chart with its dashed average and above/below tinting, the “× your average” verdicts, the best-slot rule, and the local-timezone timing charts are all from the backend — read-only, no per-post drilldown beyond the link out, no invented metrics. Mobile is a layout pass; the only structural change is the shared app shell.
States are Live / Loading / Empty — no error state by design. (The previous draft showed a shared error banner; that is removed.) Loading is a skeleton that mirrors the ready layout so nothing reflows. Empty is “Not enough data yet”.

4fixPeriod + summary fold

On desktop the 6 periods sit in one segmented row and the summary is a 4-up card grid. Both overflow a phone. The fold:

Before

Six period buttons crush below tap size or wrap raggedly; four summary cards in a row shrink their numbers until the deltas clip.

After

The period selector becomes a horizontally scrollable segmented row (all 6 reachable, ≥44px tap, active highlighted, right-edge mask fade). The summary stacks: the hero metric (Views) on its own card at text-h1 with its avg/post + delta, then a 3-up row (Posts · Likes · Comments) stepped down to text-h3 — never smaller.

Rules. Period row is overflow-x:auto with a right-edge mask fade; buttons are flex:0 0 auto, ≥36px tall (≥44 hit). Deltas are tabular: up = success, down = danger, “no prior period” for All time. The hero number uses text-h1; the 3-up cells step to text-h3 — readable at 360.

5fixThe trend chart

A bar chart of average views per bucket on a continuous, gap-filled axis: every bucket in the window is present, empty buckets render as a tiny zero stub (so a quiet stretch reads as quiet, not “two bars”). A dashed line marks the average of the posted buckets only; bars are tinted above (solid accent) vs at/below (muted). The fit strategy:

Before

A ~30-bucket month (daily) packed into 360px becomes a crushed smear — bars hairline-thin, labels overlapping into noise.

After

Short windows (≤8 buckets) fill the width responsively. Longer windows keep a readable bar width (~40px) and scroll horizontally — bars + sparse date labels stay legible, the dashed average travels with them. Never a smear.

Rules. Plot is fixed-height (156px); bars are % of the window max; empty buckets are a 2px .m-colbar--zero stub. The dashed average sits at avg ÷ max (posted-only) with an “avg N” label. Granularity is per period: hourly (Today/Yesterday) · daily (7 days/Month) · weekly (3 months/All time), in the viewer’s local time. When buckets > 8 the inner plot is width:max-content inside an overflow-x:auto wrapper with fixed 40px columns; labels go sparse (~8) so dates aren’t a wall. Otherwise bars are flex:1 and every label shows.

6newTop posts

The window’s top 5 posts by views. Each row: a rank index, a 2-line snippet (line-clamp-2), a meta line (date + views / likes / comments with small glyphs), and a right-side verdict “N× your average” — the same metric the Feed uses, colour-graded: success ≥ 1.5×, plain ≥ 0.7×, muted below. The whole row taps out to Threads while the post has a URL.

Phone rules. Two-column row (rank · body); the body holds the clamped snippet then a foot line with meta on the left (wraps) and the verdict pinned right, staying legible. Whole-row tap target (no tiny hit areas). Numbers tabular.

7newBest times to post

Two mini bar charts: By hour (local publish hour) and By day of week (Mon→Sun, the full week always drawn, empty days stubbed). Bars are avg views/post for that slot; the best slot — preferring slots with ≥ 2 posts, so one lucky post can’t crown an hour — is tinted accent and named in a “Best · …” chip. On desktop the two charts sit side-by-side; on a phone they stack, and the hour chart scrolls horizontally if it has many bars.

Local time + visibility. Hour buckets and the “Best · …” chip render in the viewer’s timezone — never the server’s. The panel is hidden entirely for Today / Yesterday (the window is too short to be meaningful) — see the period fold.

8Performance spread

The viral-tier distribution: four tiers — Viral (3×+) · Good (1.5–3×) · Average (0.7–1.5×) · Weak (below 0.7×) — each a row with a colour dot, name, faint sub-label, count, percent, and a thin proportion bar. Tier colours: viral = success, good = accent, average/weak = graded ink mixes. Rows stack full-width on a phone.

9States

loadingreadyempty

Loading mirrors the ready layout (skeleton selector + summary + chart + panels) so nothing reflows; ready is a populated period; empty is “not enough data yet”. No error state.

11Web ↔ mobile

Desktop ( > 900 )

  • Fixed sidebar; 960px column; freshness pill in the topbar.
  • Period selector in one row; summary as a 4-up card grid.
  • Trend fills width (many = denser, no scroll); best-times charts side-by-side.
  • Top posts rows + tier spread full-width.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • No sidebar/tab bar; hamburger → drawer + account; free bottom edge.
  • Period selector scrolls; summary stacks (Views hero + 3-up).
  • Trend full-width; ≤8 fill, >8 fixed 40px + scroll; best-times stack (hour scrolls).
  • Top posts + tier rows stack; meta wraps, verdict stays right.

12Localization & 360px

Period labels, card labels, the “avg” chart label, the bucket labels, the “× your average” verdict, the “Best · …” chips, tier names and delta text all localize into 8 locales (en/ru/uk/de/es/fr/it/pt); DE/RU/UK run ~30–40% longer. On a phone: the period row scrolls so longer labels stay readable; summary labels wrap inside their cell; the chart’s bucket labels are live DOM text (never baked into an image) and localize; the best-times titles/chips and tier sub-labels wrap. Numbers are tabular and locale-formatted.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — the gutter stays 14px; the period row and the wide chart already scroll, so they’re unaffected; the 3-up summary row stays 3 columns (numbers are short) and only the cell labels rewrap; the hero number and chart get ~30px narrower. The best-times “By day of week” bars hold their 7 stubs; the hour chart scrolls if needed. The drawer stays min(82%, 312px) → ≈295px at 360.

13Checklist

  • Below 600px there is no sidebar and no tab bar — one top bar with a hamburger far-left and the “Updated daily” chart-glyph pill; bottom edge free.
  • Hamburger opens the left drawer (active = Stats) + account at its foot.
  • Period selector is a horizontally scrollable segmented row; all 6 reachable; ≥44px hit; labels readable; active highlighted; right mask fade.
  • Summary stacks: hero Views (text-h1) + a 3-up row (Posts/Likes/Comments at text-h3); each metric carries its delta; All time = “no prior period”.
  • Trend is gap-filled (every bucket; empty = 2px stub); dashed average over posted buckets only; above = accent, at/below = muted; ≤8 fill, long windows keep 40px bars + scroll with sparse labels — never a smear.
  • Bucket labels localize (live text), in the viewer’s local time; granularity hour/day/week per period.
  • Top posts: top-5 by views, rank + 2-line snippet + meta + “N× your average” (success ≥1.5× / plain ≥0.7× / muted below); whole row taps to Threads.
  • Best times: By hour + By day of week (full week, empty days stubbed); best slot (≥2 posts) accent + “Best · …” chip; stacked; hour scrolls when many; hidden for Today/Yesterday.
  • Performance spread: 4 tiers (Viral/Good/Average/Weak) with count + % + bar; viral=success, good=accent, average/weak ink mixes.
  • Loading = skeleton mirroring the layout; empty = “not enough data yet”; no error state.
  • Labels wrap on long locales — never clipped; sanity-checked at 360px.
  • Correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour.
