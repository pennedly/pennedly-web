Pennedly · Mobile spec · per-screen

Stats /app/stats — phone

Account-level analytics: engagement over a chosen period, as summary numbers + charts. On a phone the charts and the period selector are what break — they overflow the width. This spec makes them fit cleanly at 390px (sanity-checked at 360): a horizontally scrollable period selector, stacked summary metrics, and a full-width bar chart that scrolls sideways when a period has many buckets so bars stay legible. Read-only, same tokens and computed numbers as the desktop spec; only the layout changes below 600px, on the decided shell (hamburger + drawer, no tab bar, free bottom edge). Frames are the real component layer at 390px, light and dark.

pennedly-mobile.css stats-app.jsx stats-parts.jsx shell-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
What does not change. The 6 fixed backend periods (Today / Yesterday / 7 days / Month / 3 months / All time), the computed summary totals + “vs previous period” deltas, the average-views-per-bucket bar chart with its dashed average line and above/below tinting, and the local-timezone hours breakdown are all from the backend — read-only, no per-post drilldown, no invented metrics. (The posts-per-week chart stays removed.) Mobile is a layout pass; the only structural change is the shared app shell.

4fixPeriod + summary fold

On desktop the 6 periods sit in one segmented row and the summary is a 4-up card grid. Both overflow a phone. The fold:

Before

Six period buttons crush below tap size or wrap raggedly; four summary cards in a row shrink their numbers until the deltas clip.

After

The period selector becomes a horizontally scrollable segmented row (all 6 reachable, labels readable, active highlighted). The summary stacks: the hero metric (Views) on its own card with its avg/post + delta, then a 3-up row (Posts · Likes · Replies) each with its delta.

Rules. Period row is overflow-x:auto with a right-edge mask fade; buttons are flex:0 0 auto, 36px tall. Deltas are tabular: up = success, down = danger, “no prior period” for All time. The hero number uses text-h1; the 3-up cells step to text-h3 — never smaller, so they stay readable.

5fixThe bar chart

A bar chart of average views per bucket: a dashed line marks the period average, and bars are tinted above (solid accent) vs below (muted) it. The fit strategy:

Before

A 12-bucket period (3 months) packed into 360px becomes a crushed smear — bars hairline-thin, labels overlapping into noise.

After

Short periods (≤8 buckets) fill the width responsively. Longer periods keep a readable bar width (40px) and scroll horizontally — bars and date labels stay legible, the dashed average travels with them. Never a smear.

Rules. The plot is fixed-height (156px); bars are % of the period max; the dashed average sits at avg ÷ max with a small “avg N” label. Bucket labels localize (weekday / date / month per the backend granularity). When buckets > 8 the inner plot is width:max-content inside an overflow-x:auto wrapper; otherwise bars are flex:1.

6Best posting hours

The hours breakdown shows when the account’s posts land best, in the viewer’s local timezone. On a phone it’s a compact bar row across the day with the peak window highlighted, plus a one-line takeaway. (The desktop’s wider panel folds to this small chart — same data, less chrome.)

Local time. Hour buckets and the “strongest window” headline render in the viewer’s timezone — never the server’s. The peak bar(s) take solid accent; the rest are muted. No posts-per-week chart (deliberately removed).

9Web ↔ mobile

Desktop ( > 900 )

  • Fixed sidebar; 960px column; freshness pill in the topbar.
  • Period selector in one row; summary as a 4-up card grid.
  • Bar chart fills width; hours panel side-by-side.

Phone ( ≤ 600 )

  • No sidebar/tab bar; hamburger → drawer + account; free bottom edge.
  • Period selector scrolls; summary stacks (hero + 3-up).
  • Bar chart full-width, scrolls when many buckets; hours panel stacks below.

10Localization & 360px

Period labels, card labels, the “avg” chart label, the hours headline/caption and delta text all localize into 8 locales; DE/RU/UK run ~30–40% longer. On a phone: the period row scrolls so longer labels stay readable; summary labels wrap inside their cell; the chart’s bucket labels are live SVG/DOM text (never baked into an image) and localize; the hours caption wraps. Numbers are tabular and locale-formatted.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — the gutter stays 14px; the period row and the wide chart already scroll, so they’re unaffected; the 3-up summary row stays 3 columns (numbers are short) and only the cell labels rewrap. The hero number and chart simply get ~30px narrower. The drawer stays min(82%, 312px) → 82% (≈295px) at 360.

11Checklist

  • Below 600px there is no sidebar and no tab bar — one 52px top bar with a hamburger far-left and the freshness pill.
  • Hamburger opens the left drawer (active = Stats) + account at its foot; bottom edge free.
  • Period selector is a horizontally scrollable segmented row; all 6 reachable; labels readable; active highlighted.
  • Summary stacks: hero Views on its own card + a 3-up row (Posts/Likes/Replies); each metric carries its delta; All time = “no prior period”.
  • Bar chart has the dashed average line + above/below tinting; short periods fill width; long periods (e.g. 3 months) scroll horizontally with readable 40px bars — never a smear.
  • Bucket labels localize (live text) and stay legible.
  • Hours breakdown shows best posting hours in the viewer’s local timezone, peak highlighted; no posts-per-week chart.
  • Loading = skeleton selector + summary + chart; empty = “not enough data yet”; error banner has Retry.
  • Labels wrap on long locales — never clipped; sanity-checked at 360px.
  • Correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour.
