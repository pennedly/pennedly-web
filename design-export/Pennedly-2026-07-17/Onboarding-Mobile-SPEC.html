Pennedly · Mobile spec · per-screen

Onboarding /app/onboarding — phone

The first-run, stepped wizard right after sign-up: connect Threads, decide how to set up your voice, let Pennedly analyze (or start from scratch), and finish. This is a full-screen flow — not inside the app shell: no sidebar, no hamburger, no tab bar. The whole screen is the wizard — a progress stepper + the current stage + a pinned primary action. Same real flow, copy and trust framing as the desktop; only the layout adapts below 600px. Frames are the real component layer at 390px, light and dark.

pennedly-mobile.css onboarding-app.jsx onboarding-parts.jsx ds/tokens.css
What does not change. Connect is real Threads OAuth. “Analyze my posts” needs a minimum post count (hence the locked state + reason). “Build from scratch” seeds the role-book from the chips/topics. The flow advances real onboarding state; the ?preview=1 variant runs for real but saves nothing (read-only). The honest trust copy — “nothing is posted without your approval” — stays. No invented steps, payment, or settings. Mobile is a layout pass; this screen has no app shell to adapt — only the wizard.

2The wizard frame

Every stage shares one frame: the brand bar, the stepper, a scrollable stage, and a footer that pins the primary action near the bottom (thumb-reachable). The stage scrolls under the fixed footer when content is tall; short stages just sit above it.

Pinned footer. The Continue / primary action lives in .m-ob-foot — a frosted bar pinned to the bottom (above the safe area), so it’s always reachable without scrolling. Secondary actions (Back, Refine) sit on the same row, left of a spacer. On the Connect step the primary grows full-width; elsewhere it pairs with a Back link.

3Stage 1 · Connect

A hero (brand mark + welcome + the trust rows) and a connect button. Sub-states: idle → connecting (spinner) → connected (a confirmation card with the account). A failed connect shows an inline error + retry.

4Stage 2 · Choose

Two big full-width option cards: “Analyze my posts” (recommended) and “Build from scratch”. If the account doesn’t have enough recent posts, Analyze is locked with a short reason and Scratch becomes the active choice.

Option cards. Each card = icon + title (+ “Recommended” or a “Needs N posts” lock) + description + a time-cost meta + a check when selected. The locked card is dimmed and non-tappable, its description explaining the post-count floor. Cards stack full-width and grow with wrapped copy.

5Analyze in progress

The analyze path animates: a nib-write mark, the account being read, and progress ticks (reading posts → finding themes → distilling how you sound). No footer action — it advances to Done on its own.

6Build from scratch

A short form to seed the voice without analysis: a “describe your voice” textarea with starter suggestions, then topic chip-fields (write about / avoid) each with tappable suggestions. The footer’s primary is “Create my voice”.

Inputs. The textarea and chip inputs are 16px (no iOS zoom). Chips wrap and stay 44px-tappable; suggestion chips add to the field on tap; entered chips carry a remove ✕. The form scrolls under the pinned footer.

7Done · preview

Done recaps what got set up (connected account + how the voice was built) and sends you into Studio. The tester preview (?preview=1) instead shows the voice Pennedly would build — read-only — with a “run setup for real” action.

9Localization & 360px

Titles, subs, trust copy, option titles/descriptions, field labels, suggestion chips and button copy all localize into 8 locales; DE/RU/UK run ~30–40% longer. On a phone: titles and descriptions wrap freely; the stepper labels are short but the nodes hold if a label wraps; option-card titles wrap above their meta; “Connect Threads account” / “Create my voice” / “Go to Studio” wrap rather than clip in the footer; chips and suggestions wrap. The trust line (“nothing is posted without your approval”) always stays visible.

360px behavior. No new breakpoint — stage gutters stay 18px; the hero, option cards and form just get ~30px narrower so copy rewraps to more lines. The pinned footer keeps its 44px controls; on a long-locale footer the primary wraps to two lines (still reachable). The stepper stays a single row (labels are short in every locale). The drawer doesn’t exist here.

10Checklist

  • No app shell — no sidebar, hamburger, or tab bar. A full-screen wizard: brand bar + stepper + stage + pinned footer.
  • Stepper shows Connect · Voice · Done; current accent, completed = success check.
  • Connect: idle → connecting (spinner) → connected (confirmation card); a failed connect shows an inline error + retry.
  • Choose: two full-width option cards; Analyze is locked with a reason when there aren’t enough posts, and Scratch becomes active.
  • Analyze animates the nib-write + progress ticks; advances to Done on its own.
  • From scratch: 16px textarea + starters, topic chip-fields (write / avoid) with tappable suggestions; chips 44px and wrap.
  • Done recaps the account + voice and goes to Studio; ?preview=1 shows the read-only voice preview + “run setup for real”.
  • The primary action is pinned near the bottom (thumb-reachable), safe-area aware.
  • Trust copy (“nothing is posted without your approval”) is present.
  • Copy wraps on long locales — never clipped; sanity-checked at 360px.
  • Correct in light and dark; no hardcoded colour.
